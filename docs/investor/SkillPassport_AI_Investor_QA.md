# SkillPassport AI — Investor Q&A Guide

This guide covers the most critical questions investors will ask during a pitch session, along with honest, investor-friendly answers, strategic questions to ask investors, and key advice for founder handling.

---

> **Why this matters:** Q&A is often more important than the pitch itself. Investors spend **5–10 minutes listening** and **20–30 minutes asking questions**. They're not trying to embarrass you—they're testing whether you understand your business.

---

## 1. "In one sentence, what are you building?"

### Best Answer
> **"SkillPassport AI is building the trusted digital identity and infrastructure for technical talent, where verified work replaces traditional resumes and connects developers with companies, universities, investors, and opportunities."**

---

## 2. "Isn't this just another recruitment platform?"

### Why they ask
They want to see if you have a unique position.

### Best Answer
> "Recruitment is only one application of our platform. Our core product is the Skill Passport—a lifelong, verified professional identity built from real contributions. Recruitment is simply one way that identity creates value."

---

## 3. "Why would anyone leave LinkedIn or GitHub?"

### Best Answer
> "We don't want them to. GitHub is the home for code, and LinkedIn is the home for networking. SkillPassport connects verified achievements from multiple sources into one trusted professional identity. We complement existing platforms instead of replacing them."

---

## 4. "What problem are you solving?"

### Best Answer
> "The biggest problem isn't finding developers—it's trusting them. Today, technical evidence is scattered across many platforms, making hiring slow and inefficient. We unify verified work into one trusted identity."

---

## 5. "Who is your customer?"

### Best Answer
> "Initially, our paying customers are technology companies hiring early-career developers. Our users are developers, but our first revenue comes from companies looking for faster and more reliable hiring."

---

## 6. "What will you build first?"

*(This is one of the most important questions.)*

### Don't say
> "Everything."

### Say
> "Phase 1 focuses on three things:
> * **Skill Passport**
> * **Verified project portfolio**
> * **Recruiter dashboard**
> 
> Everything else is part of our long-term roadmap."

---

## 7. "How do you make money?"

### Best Answer
> "Our first revenue comes from enterprise recruiter subscriptions. As the platform grows, we'll expand into premium developer services, sponsored technical challenges, and marketplace partnerships."

*(Keep it simple.)*

---

## 8. "What makes you different?"

### Best Answer
> "Existing platforms solve individual problems. We connect the entire developer journey into one trusted identity. Our differentiation isn't a feature—it's the professional identity layer."

---

## 9. "What is Proof of Skill?"

### Best Answer
> "Proof of Skill is our long-term vision for verifying technical ability through evidence instead of claims. It combines verified projects, deployments, technical challenges, certifications, and professional contributions into a trusted record that organizations can rely on."

*(Don't claim it's already an industry standard.)*

---

## 10. "Why would companies trust your score?"

### Best Answer
> "We don't ask companies to trust a black-box score. We provide transparent evidence behind every profile—projects, deployments, challenge results, and verified contributions—so organizations can make informed decisions."

---

## 11. "How will you get your first 10,000 users?"

### Best Answer
> "We'll start with engineering colleges and developer communities, offering free Skill Passports and portfolio hosting. Once we establish a strong developer base, we onboard companies looking for verified early-career talent."

---

## 12. "How will you compete with companies that have billions of users?"

### Best Answer
> "We're not competing on scale from day one. We're solving a specific problem they don't own: unified technical identity. We focus on one niche, validate it, and expand gradually."

---

## 13. "What if GitHub launches the same feature?"

### Best Answer
> "GitHub owns repositories, not professional identity across the entire ecosystem. Our platform integrates projects, internships, certifications, deployments, challenges, universities, and hiring. Our value comes from connecting the ecosystem, not from one feature."

---

## 14. "Why now?"

### Best Answer
> "Hiring is shifting from resume-based evaluation to evidence-based hiring. Companies increasingly value practical skills, open-source contributions, and deployed projects. The industry is ready for a trusted identity layer."

---

## 15. "What's your biggest risk?"

### Best Answer
> "Adoption. That's why we're not launching as a full marketplace. We first create value for developers with Skill Passports and verified portfolios before expanding to companies and other stakeholders."

*(Investors appreciate founders who recognize real risks.)*

---

## 16. "How much funding do you need?"

### Best Answer
> "We're seeking enough funding to build and validate Phase 1 over the next 12–18 months, focusing on product development, infrastructure, early partnerships, and customer validation. The exact amount depends on the scope agreed with our investors."

*(If they ask for a number, give one that matches your plan.)*

---

## 17. "What's your long-term vision?"

### Best Answer
> "Our long-term vision is to make SkillPassport the trusted professional identity for technical talent worldwide, similar to how GitHub became the standard for code collaboration."

---

## 18. "Why are you the right team?"

*(This is where many founders struggle.)*

### Best Answer
> "We're technical students who have personally experienced fragmented portfolios, repeated verification, and hiring challenges. We're building a platform to solve a problem we've lived ourselves, and our technical background allows us to build and iterate quickly."

*(Don't claim you're experts in everything. Show you understand the problem deeply.)*

---

## 19. "If I give you ₹2 crore tomorrow, what happens?"

### Best Answer
> "We'll focus on one outcome: proving that a verified Skill Passport improves developer visibility and helps companies hire more efficiently. Success for us is product-market validation, not building every planned feature."

---

## 20. "Why should I invest in you?"

### Best Answer
> "Because we're not trying to build another recruitment platform. We're building the trust layer that connects the fragmented developer ecosystem. If we succeed, recruitment becomes just one application of a much larger identity network."

---

# Questions You Should Ask the Investors

A good pitch isn't one-sided. At the end, you can ask:

* **"What concerns you most about this idea?"**
* **"If you were building this company, what would you focus on first?"**
* **"Do you see this as a recruitment company or an identity infrastructure company?"**
* **"What milestone would convince you that we've found product-market fit?"**

*(These questions show you're coachable and focused on building the right business.)*

---

# One Final Piece of Advice

**Don't defend every idea.** If an investor says:

> *"I think your cloud marketplace is unnecessary."*

Don't argue. Instead, say:

> **"That's valuable feedback. The cloud marketplace is part of our long-term vision, but our immediate focus is the Skill Passport and identity layer. We're disciplined about building one core product before expanding."**

That answer demonstrates focus and maturity—qualities investors often value as much as the idea itself.
